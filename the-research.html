Social media addiction is a documented public health crisis.
SocialsCap is our response.

Verslaving aan sociale media is een gedocumenteerde volksgezondheidscrisis.
SocialsCap is ons antwoord.

The major platforms are engineered to be addictive. Variable reward schedules, infinite scroll, and algorithmically-optimised feeds exploit the same neurological mechanisms as slot machines. The evidence is clear — and growing. SocialsCap was built as a serious, privacy-respecting intervention: a hard limit that actually holds.

De grote platforms zijn ontworpen om verslavend te zijn. Variabele beloningsschema's, eindeloos scrollen en algoritmisch geoptimaliseerde feeds maken gebruik van dezelfde neurologische mechanismen als gokautomaten. Het bewijs is duidelijk — en groeit. SocialsCap is gebouwd als een serieuze, privacyrespecterende interventie: een harde limiet die écht werkt.

Book · 2024

The Anxious Generation

Jonathan Haidt — social psychologist, NYU Stern School of Business

Haidt documents the sharp decline in adolescent mental health that began around 2012 — precisely when social media became smartphone-native. He argues that the "great rewiring of childhood" by social apps is a primary driver of the teen mental health crisis, and calls for hard limits on platform access.

anxiousgeneration.com
Book · 2017

iGen

Jean Twenge — professor of psychology, San Diego State University

Twenge's longitudinal analysis of generational survey data showed that teens who spend more time on social media are significantly more likely to report depression, loneliness, and sleep disruption. The research established the correlation between screen time and mental health decline that has since been replicated widely.

simonandschuster.com
Research · Ongoing

Variable reward schedules and compulsive use

B.F. Skinner's operant conditioning — applied to platform design

Social media feeds are built on variable ratio reinforcement — the same psychological mechanism that makes slot machines compelling. Unpredictable likes, comments, and viral moments create a neurological reward loop that resists voluntary interruption. This is not accidental design; it is intentional.

ScienceDirect — Compulsive social media use
Documentary · 2020

The Social Dilemma

Netflix — featuring Tristan Harris, Aza Raskin, and former platform engineers

Former engineers and ethicists from Google, Facebook, Twitter, and Pinterest explain in detail how recommendation algorithms and notification systems are designed to maximise engagement at the expense of user wellbeing. One of the most-watched documents on tech's impact on mental health.

thesocialdilemma.com
Book · 2019

Digital Minimalism

Cal Newport — professor of computer science, Georgetown University

Newport argues that passive scrolling of social feeds provides little genuine value while consuming significant time and attention. He proposes intentional, time-bounded use as the only sustainable alternative to compulsive checking — the exact approach SocialsCap enables.

calnewport.com
Research · 2017

Social Media Addiction Scale (SMAS-6)

Andreassen et al. — University of Bergen

The Bergen Social Media Addiction Scale, published in Psychological Reports, provides a validated six-item scale to assess addictive social media use — preoccupation, tolerance, withdrawal, relapse, conflict, and problems. It established that social media addiction meets diagnostic criteria for behavioural addiction.

Our design philosophy

Onze ontwerpfilosofie

Why SocialsCap is different

Waarom SocialsCap anders is

Most screen time tools rely on awareness and nudges — gentle reminders that are trivially easy to dismiss. For users in the grip of compulsive use, awareness is not the problem. SocialsCap is built on a different premise.

De meeste schermtijdtools vertrouwen op bewustzijn en nudges — zachte herinneringen die triviaal eenvoudig te negeren zijn. Voor gebruikers die gevangen zitten in compulsief gebruik is bewustzijn niet het probleem. SocialsCap is gebouwd op een ander uitgangspunt.

Hard limits, not reminders

When the daily limit is reached, apps are blocked. Not nudged. Not warned. Blocked. This is intentional — a nudge is what the platforms themselves use to keep you engaged; it is not a credible counter-intervention.

PIN protection removes the escape hatch

Awareness tools fail because the same device that shows the warning is the device that launches the app. Premium PIN protection means a trusted person — not the user — holds the key to extending limits. This is the critical difference between a reminder and an intervention.

Zero data, zero conflict of interest

SocialsCap collects no personal data, uses no analytics, has no advertising, and requires no account. Our only revenue is the €3.99 one-time Premium upgrade. We have no financial incentive to keep you on your phone — the opposite of every platform we're limiting.

Built for serious use cases

SocialsCap was not designed as a productivity app for casual use. It was designed for people who recognise they have a problem and want a tool that takes that seriously — and for institutions, therapists, and parents supporting someone who does.

Harde limieten, geen herinneringen

Wanneer de daglimiet bereikt is, worden apps geblokkeerd. Niet gewaarschuwd. Niet herinnerd. Geblokkeerd. Dit is opzettelijk — een nudge is wat de platforms zelf gebruiken om je betrokken te houden.

PIN-beveiliging sluit de ontsnappingsroute

Een vertrouwenspersoon — niet de gebruiker zelf — beheert de sleutel om limieten te verlengen. Dit is het cruciale verschil tussen een herinnering en een echte interventie.

Nul data, nul belangenconflict

SocialsCap verzamelt geen persoonsgegevens, gebruikt geen analytics, heeft geen advertenties en vereist geen account. Onze enige inkomstenbron is de eenmalige €3,99 Premium upgrade.

Gebouwd voor serieus gebruik

SocialsCap is ontworpen voor mensen die erkennen dat ze een probleem hebben en een tool willen die dat serieus neemt — en voor instellingen, therapeuten en ouders die iemand ondersteunen.
